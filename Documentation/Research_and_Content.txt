LEAD by ELEVATE – PART 1 RESEARCH AND CONTENT BASIS

Research evidence used for the website:
• The supplied WEDE5020 ICE Task 1.
• The supplied LEAD photographs and promotional graphics.
• The supplied Member Expectations material.
• The supplied coaching series material.
• The LEAD proposal content prepared for Part 1.

Key themes identified in the supplied LEAD material:
• Leadership development
• Personal mastery and leadership readiness
• Mentorship and exposure
• Industry site visits, talks and shadowing
• Community and belonging
• Peer learning and opportunity sharing
• Long-term career growth
• Professional conduct and respect
• Character, competence and contribution
• Coaching, including mental wellness, career growth and operations

The Member Expectations material also distinguishes what LEAD is not:
• Not a job placement agency
• Not a shortcut to success
• Not a free-for-all network
• Not about titles or status

Content caution:
The website does not invent official phone numbers, email addresses, legal claims or live event dates that were not supplied. Any future public contact information should be confirmed by the organisation before publication.
