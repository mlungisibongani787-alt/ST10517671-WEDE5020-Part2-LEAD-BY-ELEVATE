LEAD by ELEVATE – PART 1 PROPOSAL SUMMARY

PROPOSAL 1 – SIMPLE INFORMATION WEBSITE
Purpose: Create a clear website that introduces LEAD by Elevate and explains its leadership-development purpose.
Main pages: Home, About, Services, Enquiry, Contact Us and Gallery.
Advantages: Simple navigation, clear information architecture, supplied LEAD photographs, and a suitable foundation for Part 1.

PROPOSAL 2 – EVENT AND COMMUNITY WEBSITE
Purpose: Create a website focused on upcoming sessions, member activities, coaching and community interaction.
Main pages: Home, About, Events, Resources, Enquiry and Gallery.
Advantages: Strong event focus and potential for future expansion.

SELECTED PROPOSAL
Proposal 1 – LEAD by Elevate is selected for the website. The Part 1 website is intentionally kept simple. Professional branding, advanced styling, responsive refinement and additional functionality can be developed in Part 2.
