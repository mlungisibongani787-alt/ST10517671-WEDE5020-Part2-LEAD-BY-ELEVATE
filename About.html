About LEAD by ELEVATE

A development platform for young professionals and emerging leaders.

Who we are

Development with character, competence and purpose

LEAD by ELEVATE is a development platform and community focused on helping young professionals and emerging leaders build capability, professional confidence and meaningful connections.

Its activities bring together leadership development, mentorship, exposure, learning and community engagement.

Mission

To create practical opportunities for leadership development, mentorship, exposure, learning and professional connection.

Vision

To contribute to a community of future-ready professionals who lead with character, competence and purpose.

Values

Character, competence and contribution guide the way members learn, participate and support one another.

Member expectations

What participation looks like

  • Remain open to learning and development.
  • Demonstrate professional conduct and respect.
  • Engage actively in the community.
  • Contribute knowledge, opportunities and support.
What LEAD is not: a job placement agency, a shortcut to success, a free-for-all network, or primarily about titles and status.
