About LEAD by Elevate

Who we are

LEAD by Elevate is a development platform and community focused on helping young professionals and emerging leaders build capability, professional confidence and meaningful connections.

Mission

To create practical opportunities for leadership development, mentorship, exposure, learning and professional connection.

Vision

To contribute to a community of future-ready professionals who lead with character, competence and purpose.

What LEAD is not

  • Not a job placement agency.
  • Not a shortcut to success.
  • Not a free-for-all network.
  • Not primarily about titles or status.

Member expectations

Members are expected to remain open to learning, demonstrate professional conduct and respect, engage actively and contribute to a people-first community.

What LEAD values

  • Character: Respect, professionalism and responsible conduct.
  • Competence: Continuous learning and practical development.
  • Contribution: Sharing knowledge, opportunities and support with others.
