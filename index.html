What LEAD is about

A practical community for professional growth

The website presents the organisation's core activities and the development opportunities described in the selected Part 1 proposal.

Leadership Development

Practical sessions that support personal mastery, leadership readiness and professional growth.

Mentorship & Exposure

Guidance, career conversations, industry insights and opportunities to learn from experienced professionals.

Community & Belonging

Coffee sessions, peer learning and meaningful connections that encourage contribution and support.

Member expectations

Come ready to learn and contribute

LEAD encourages members to remain open to learning, demonstrate professional conduct and respect, engage actively and contribute to a people-first community.

  • Character, respect and professional conduct
  • Continuous learning and practical development
  • Sharing knowledge, opportunities and support
